Implement Daily Cash Interest Accumulation for Backtesting

1. Core Feature: Daily Cash Interest Attribution:
- Modified `src/backtesting/portfolio.py`: Added `add_cash_interest(daily_rate)` method to allow the portfolio to earn interest on idle cash balances.
- Updated `src/training/run_optimization.py`: Integrated daily interest accumulation into the main backtest optimization loop using the real-world risk-free rate.
- Updated `run_selection_only_backtest.py`: Added interest accumulation to the selection-only benchmark to ensure parity with the full pipeline.

2. Continuous Compounding Support:
- Enabled continuous compounding by applying the daily risk-free rate (sourced from `daily_risk_free_rates.parquet`) to the cash balance at the end of every trading day.
- This results in more realistic long-term portfolio value trajectories, especially for 9-year backtests.

3. Data Verification & Logging:
- Verified the integrity of the risk-free rate data source; confirmed rates are correctly fetched even when near zero (e.g., early 2016) and correctly reflect positive yield in later years (e.g., 2023 at ~5% annual).
- Enhanced backtest logs to include "Interest added to cash" events for better auditability of portfolio value growth.

Verified interest attribution in 2023 samples showing correct daily compounding.
